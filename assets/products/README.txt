Put your product photos in this folder.

Recommended: square images (e.g. 800x800px), .jpg or .png, under 500KB each
for fast loading.

Then in script.js, find the product in the PRODUCTS array and set its
"image" field to the file path, e.g.:

  image: "assets/products/strawberries.jpg",

Leave it as "" to keep the illustrated placeholder for that product.
